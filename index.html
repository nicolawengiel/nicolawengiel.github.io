Nicola Wengiel

I am a mathematics PhD student in the Mathematical Data Analytics group at FAU Erlangen–Nürnberg, advised by Timm Oertel. I expect to complete my PhD in 2027 and am looking for a postdoctoral position starting later that year.

My research focuses on discrepancy theory, especially online vector balancing and geometric discrepancy. I also work on random matrix discrepancy and Davenport constants.

My background is in mathematical optimization, and my master’s thesis at FAU focused on mixed-integer programming. See my CV for details.
